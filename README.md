# voitta-lab

Umbrella repo for tooling, prototypes, and anything worth discussing in public.

Issues here are the public counterpart to work tracked privately elsewhere.
Findings that are generally useful — reproductions, verification runs, gotchas
in other people's software — get filed here so they can be linked from blog
posts and shared without exposing private context.

Prototypes live here too, until they earn their own repo.

## Conventions

- No account IDs, client names, or infra topology. Genericize to placeholders;
  the technique is what's reusable.
- Issues should stand on their own: enough detail to reproduce, without
  requiring access to anything private.
